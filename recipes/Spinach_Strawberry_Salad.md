tags: ["Salads", "Picnic Fare", "Side dish", "Kim Acceptable"]
servings: "Servings: 4-6"
---
**Ingredients:**

*Dressing:*

1/2 cup sugar

1 Tbsp Poppy Seeds

2 Tbsps Sesame Seeds, toasted

1/2 tsp minced onion

1/4 tsp paprika

1/4 cup cider vinegar

1/4 cup olive oil

*Salad:*

3/4 cup slivered almonds sauteed with 2 Tbsps butter

1 pound torn spinach cleaned well.

1 pint strawberries sliced thinly.

Toss right before serving.
